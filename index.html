A Beginner's Guide to Ice Hockey


Basic Info


  • Each puck that crosses the goal line counts as a 1 goal.
  • You need to score more goals than your opponent, which can be as small as one goal if the opposing team has 0 (a shutout)
  • A singular win is worth 2 points in the NHL, but 3 points in international hockey.

Ice Hockey Equipment


  • Hockey Stick.
  • Hockey Gear (helmet, kneepads, elbow pads, etc.)
  • Ice Skates

Basic Techniques


  • Skating (bent knees, clean c cuts; navigate sharp turns).
  • Stickhandling (loose & flexible grip, forehand & backhand; bypass defenders).
  • Shooting (wrist shot, snap shot, slap shot).
